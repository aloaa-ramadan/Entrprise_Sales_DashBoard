# 📊 Enterprise Sales Dashboard | Tableau

An interactive **Tableau** dashboard project analyzing enterprise sales data, covering key performance indicators such as quantity, revenue, profit margin, and discounts, with full filtering and interactivity.

---

## 🖼️ Overview

The project consists of **2 main dashboards**:

### 1️⃣ Detailed Enterprise Sales Dashboard
A detailed dashboard focused on product, pricing, and profitability analysis.

**Components:**
- **Quantity by Category** – Bar chart showing quantity sold per category (Accessories, Electronics, Hardware, Home Appliances, Office Supplies)
- **WildCard Table** – Interactive table for viewing custom data by name
- **FinalPrice & Profit Trend** – Line chart tracking final price and profit trends month by month across the year
- **Total Revenue by ProductName (Top N)** – Top-performing products by revenue, with a Top N filter to control how many are displayed
- **Profit Margin by Region** – Pie chart showing profit margin distribution across regions (East Coast, West Coast, North, South, Midwest)

**Available Filters:**
- Select Measure
- Top N Products
- Category
- Show/Hide WildCard

---

### 2️⃣ Enterprise Sales Dashboard Overview
A high-level dashboard providing a broader view of performance by geography, supplier, and gender.

**Components:**
- **Orders by State** – Map chart showing order counts by U.S. state (Washington, Michigan, New York, Illinois, Colorado...)
- **FinalPrice by Category** – Treemap showing final price/revenue distribution across categories
- **Discount by Gender** – Pie chart showing discount distribution by gender (Male/Female)
- **Quantity by Supplier** – Bar chart showing quantity sold per supplier
- **Orders per Category** – Combo chart (bar + line) showing order counts per category

**Available Filters:**
- State
- Supplier Name
- Show Quantity by Supplier
- Gender

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Tableau Desktop / Public**
- **Data Cleaning & Preparation** (prior to import)
- **Interactive Filters & Parameters**
- **Custom Color Palette** (Gold / Orange / Brown theme)

---

## 🎯 Key Insights
- The **Electronics** category leads in both quantity sold and revenue.
- The **Midwest** region achieves the highest profit margin (24.1%) compared to other regions.
- Discount distribution is nearly balanced between genders (51.7% Male vs. 48.3% Female).
- States like **New York** and **Michigan** lead in total order counts.
